Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ShortsPipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it reads read-only YouTube data and YouTube Analytics for the owner's own channel: video titles, IDs and performance metrics such as views and average view duration.

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure how the owner's own videos perform and to adjust future edits. It is stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by this tool.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
